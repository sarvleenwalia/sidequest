# Research notes

Initial desk research: 6 October 2026. This is a small source library, not an exhaustive literature review. Official summaries and course archives were read; student work has not been independently reproduced. Papers were inspected at abstract/task-description level, not fully replicated.

| Source | Actual work | Transferable structure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [MIT 6.824, 2014](https://css.csail.mit.edu/6.824/2014/projects.html) | Merge distributed editor; Barista relational store; distributed filesystems and consensus projects | Build a bounded system; define promises; expose failure; evaluate tradeoffs. Graduate team scope must be reduced for teens. |

| [Lost in the Middle, 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03172) | Controlled evaluation of information position in long model contexts | Isolate a variable and test model failure. Older findings must be retested on current models. |

## Generation principles inferred from these sources

1. Specific questions beat broad domains.
2. Artifacts need a way to inspect their claims.
3. Baselines, alternative interpretations, and known solutions create useful comparisons.
4. Data access, rights, compute, supervision, and time determine feasible scope.
5. A smaller reproduction plus a justified extension is a valid learning project. It is not automatically a novel contribution.
6. Digital humanities require source criticism and substantive interpretation, not just counts or visual polish.
