Each square is one donor. Feature selection and scaling are fitted using only training-fold profiles. The whole-dataset embedding is never used as model input.

Uncertainty, kept visible

The percentile interval resamples whole donors from fixed out-of-fold predictions. Models are not refitted. This conditional interval does not capture external-cohort uncertainty or demonstrate clinical reliability.

A simple baseline first

Variance filtering → training-fold gene selection → standardization → regularized logistic regression.

Compare balanced accuracy with a majority baseline. A sophisticated model would need to earn its complexity on independently verified data. No novel model architecture is claimed.

The wedge
Help stem-cell research teams decide whether a single-cell dataset supports a prediction claim, then produce a reproducible evidence report.

What could make it valuable

Review study design earlier. Give collaborators a report they can inspect and reproduce. Make limitations visible beside results.

The current model is standard open-source machinery. A potential advantage would need to come from trustworthy metadata adapters and demonstrated workflow value. There is no proven moat today.

Milestones that would earn a stronger pitch

Three lab review exercises

Observe the actual workflow; measure time and design problems found against an expert notebook baseline.

An eligible donor cohort

Confirm donor, time point, genotype, batch and cell type. Freeze the task and analysis plan before evaluation.

Independent validation and a paid-pilot decision

Test a fixed model on a compatible external cohort, then decide whether the workflow justifies hosted collaboration.

Questions a serious reviewer will ask

Is the algorithm novel?

No. Logistic regression is a standard baseline. The proposed product is an evidence-review workflow; its usefulness and differentiation must still be demonstrated.

Does this predict future cell fate or early Parkinson’s vulnerability?

No. Future fate needs lineage or longitudinal outcome labels. Real disease generalization needs independently replicated donors and separate validation. Neither has been demonstrated here.

Why won’t a lab just use Scanpy or Seurat?

They may. Those are established tools. StemScope must show that its focused study-review workflow is easier to reproduce or catches meaningful design problems compared with existing notebooks.

What is the current ask?

Introductions to research collaborators and design partners. Funding amount, market size, traction and customer savings are not invented.
